# Resubmission document map

Date: 31 August 2026

The journal resubmission package uses a combined supplementary document. The
revised scientific validation material is followed by the original operational
guide and its three supplementary figures. Journal Word files are distribution
artifacts and are not versioned in this source repository; the underlying
scripts, benchmark data, and machine-readable results remain versioned here.

## Combined supplementary contents

- Supplementary Tables S1-S11 contain the functional comparison, harmonization
  audit, nominated-variant audit, source sample counts, genomic-inflation
  results, official-tool agreement, power calculations, HaploReg checks,
  LD-selector validation, common-association clumping audit, and optional
  AI-interface evaluation.
- Supplementary Table S12 is the separately supplied machine-readable workbook
  of 103 common-association loci and 35 fields. It was the table referenced by
  the original supplementary document but was not embedded in that Word file.
- Supplementary File S1 contains the exact natural-language prompts, scoring
  rules, run-level results, remediation substitutions, and explicit-spec
  diagnostics.
- Supplementary Figures S1-S3 are retained from the original supplementary
  document: the descriptive GTEx lookup, the representative common-hit local
  Manhattan panel, and the representative local GTF view.
- The appended operational guide covers cross-platform installation, direct
  command-line execution, SAS ODA rendering, local gnuplot rendering, and the
  optional MCP-compatible interface.

The combined Word supplement was structurally checked as 17 pages, 12 embedded
Word tables, and three embedded figures. The clean manuscript, tracked
manuscript, and response letter cross-reference the combined supplement and
Supplementary Table S12. A package-local SHA-256 inventory accompanies the
journal files.
